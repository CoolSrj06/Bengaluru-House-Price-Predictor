Actual vs Predicted

This plot compares actual and predicted prices. Most points lie close to the diagonal line, indicating good predictions. Some deviation exists for higher-priced houses.

Residual Plot

Residuals represent prediction errors. Errors increase for higher values, indicating reduced stability for expensive properties.

Price Distribution

The dataset is right-skewed, meaning most houses are in lower price ranges. This affects model performance on high-value properties.
